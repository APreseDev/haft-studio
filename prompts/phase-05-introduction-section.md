"We have only just begun; every project we undertake is the most important project of the year."

## Requirements

- Determine whether the section should be retained, redesigned, or removed.

- Do not remove it without evaluating its importance.
- If retained, improve its visual presentation.
- Replace the existing golden gradient with a more appropriate color combination.

- Preserve the website's current overall color palette.

Make a thoughtful design decision based on the actual website rather than introducing unnecessary changes.

Explain your decision briefly, implement the necessary changes, and report the modified files.

Do not modify unrelated sections or start future phases.
